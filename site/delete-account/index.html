Account closed

Your LoadQ account is now closed and your personal profile has been erased. Any records we must keep by law are anonymized and permanently deleted within 30 days.

Votre compte LoadQ est fermé et votre profil personnel a été effacé. Les documents que la loi nous oblige à conserver sont anonymisés puis supprimés définitivement sous 30 jours.

What gets deleted

Immediately: your name, phone number, email, profile photo, date of birth, and payout (Interac) details. Your login is disabled so the account can't be used again.

  • Driver and/or passenger profile
  • Contact details and profile photo
  • Identity documents (removed within 30 days)
  • Saved notification tokens

What we keep, and for how long

Trip and payment records are kept with your personal identifiers removed, only as long as Canadian tax and consumer-protection law requires (up to 7 years), after which they are permanently deleted. We keep them to meet legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention obligations.

Les dossiers de trajets et de paiements sont conservés sans vos identifiants personnels, uniquement le temps exigé par les lois fiscales et de protection du consommateur (jusqu'à 7 ans), puis supprimés définitivement.

Prefer help?

You can also request deletion by emailing support@loadq.ca from your account's email. We'll action it within 30 days.
