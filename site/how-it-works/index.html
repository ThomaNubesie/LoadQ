HOW LOADQ WORKS.

From opening the app to arriving in the next city. Four steps. No mystery.

// PASSENGER FLOW

From your couch to the car.

01

Open LoadQ

Sign in with email — we send a one-time code. No password. The board loads instantly with every active zone near you.

→
02

Pick a zone

Tap your route. See how many cars are queued, how full each one is, what time each driver wants to leave.

→
03

Watch it fill

Stay home and watch. When a car hits 80% full, you get a push — that's when to head out.

→
04

Walk up. Go.

Show up at the loading point. Pay the driver. Get in. The car leaves when full.

// DRIVER FLOW

How drivers use it.

01

Register

Upload your licence, insurance, and vehicle photos. We verify within 24 hours.

→
02

List a route

Pick zone + destination + departure window. Set your per-seat price. Hit publish.

→
03

Confirm & drive

When your seats are full, confirm in-app. Drive. Payout lands the next morning.

// FOR THE DETAIL-MINDED

Common questions.

Can I reserve a seat ahead of time?
Yes. Tap any car on the board to hold a seat for up to 30 minutes before departure. Or skip the reservation and just walk up — the choice is yours.
How do I pay?
Cash to the driver, Interac e-Transfer in-app, or card via Stripe. The driver chooses what they accept; you see it before you reserve.
What if no car fills?
You haven't paid anything, so you've lost nothing. Walk to the next loading car, or check the board again later — most zones see multiple cars per hour.
Is there a passenger app for drivers?
Same app, different mode. You can be a passenger today and a driver tomorrow if you're verified for both.
