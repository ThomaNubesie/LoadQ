About LoadQ

BUILT ON A
REAL DRIVE.

LoadQ is a Concord Express Inc. product. We made it because we drove the corridor ourselves — and we were tired of bus-station waits.

"One night in late 2024, I drove Québec → Montréal → Ottawa → Toronto → London in a single push. Every leg, I picked up someone who had been waiting hours for a bus that was already half full when it arrived. The information was the missing piece — not the seats, not the willingness to share a ride. LoadQ is that information, made visible." — Derick · Founder
