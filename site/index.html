// THE LOADQ PROMISE

Three things we'll never do.

01 · COST

NO FEES.

Pay only the driver, only the ride. No platform cut, no service charge, no surprises at the door. Currently free during our soft launch.

02 · FREEDOM

NO ENGAGEMENT.

No accounts to chase, no penalties, no commitments. Reserve a seat with a tap — or just walk up to a loading car and go.

03 · VISIBILITY

NO GUESSING.

Open LoadQ from anywhere and watch the queue fill in real time. Leave home when the car is almost full. Save the bus-station wait.
